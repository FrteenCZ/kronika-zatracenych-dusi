> [!example]
> ${}(a_{n}){}$ kde ${}a_{n}=\frac{1}{n}{}$ je couchyovksá v ${}\mathbb{R}{}$
> 
> $$
> | a_{n}-a_{m} | = |\frac{1}{n}-\frac{1}{m}| \leq |\frac{1}{n}|+| \frac{1}{m}| = \frac{1}{n}+\frac{1}{m}<\epsilon
> $$
> 
> $$
> \begin{align}
> \frac{\frac{1}{n}<\epsilon}{2} && \frac{\frac{1}{m}<\epsilon}{2} \\
> \frac{2}{\epsilon}<n && \vdots \\
> n_{01}=\max \left\{  1,\left[ \frac{2}{\epsilon} \right]  \right\}  && n_{02}\max \left\{  1,\left[ \frac{2}{\epsilon} \right]  \right\} \\
> \text{ pro } n>né1 & \implies \frac{1}{n}< \frac{\epsilon}{2} && \text{pro } n>n_{02} \implies \frac{1}{m} < \frac{\epsilon}{2}
> \end{align}
> $$
> pro ${}n_{0}=\max \left\{  1,\left[ \frac{2}{\epsilon} \right]  \right\}{}$
> pro ${}m,n>n_{0}\implies \frac{1}{n}< \frac{\epsilon}{2}\land \frac{1}{m}< \frac{\epsilon}{2}{}$

Pozn.: Definici cauchyovskosti kde 
$$
\forall\epsilon>0~~~~\exists n_{0}\in \mathbb{N}~~~~\forall n,p \in \mathbb{N}:~~~~n>n_{0}\implies|a_{n}-a_{n+p}|<\epsilon
$$

> [!quote] Lemma (Couchyovskost a omezenost)
> Pokud je ${}(a_{n}){}$ cauchyovksá ${}\implies(a_{n}){}$ je omezená

> [!done] Důkaz
> předp. ${}\forall\epsilon>0~~~\exists n_{0}\in \mathbb{N}~~~\forall n,m\in \mathbb{N}{}$

> [!quote] Věta (Nutná a postačující podmínka konvergence, Bolzano-Cauchyova věta)
> ${}(a_{n}){}$ je konvergetní v ${}\mathbb{R}\Leftrightarrow(a_{n}){}$ je cauchyovksá v ${}\mathbb{R}{}$

> [!done] Důkaz
> - ${}\boxed{\implies}~\exists a\in \mathbb{R}~~\forall\epsilon>0~~\exists n_{0}\in \mathbb{N}~~~\forall n\in \mathbb{N}{}$ 
>   chceme: ${}\forall\epsilon>0\exists n_{0}\in \mathbb{N}:~\forall m,n\in \mathbb{N}: {}$


> [!example] Příklad důležitý
> ${}(s_{n}){}$ kde ${}s_{n}=\sum_{k=1}^n \frac{1}{k}{}$ - součet prvních ${}n{}$ členů posl. ${}(a_{n}){}$ kde ${}a_{n}=\frac{1}{n}{}$
> 
> $$
> \begin{align}
> &s_{1}=1 \\
> &s_{2}=1+\frac{1}{2} \\
> &s_{3}=1+\frac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{3} \\
> & \vdots
> \end{align}
> $$
> 
> **Otázka**: Je ${}(s_{n}){}$ konvergentní? ==🔴NE==
> Ukážeme, že ${}(s_{n}){}$ **není cauchyovská**
> $$
> \exists\epsilon>0~~\forall n_{0}\in \mathbb{N}~~~\exists m,n\in \mathbb{N}: n>n_{0} \land m>n_{0}\land |s_{n}-s_{m}|\geq\epsilon
> $$
> $$
> \begin{align}
> |s_{n}-s_{m}|=|\sum_{k=1}^n \frac{1}{k}-\sum_{k=1}^m \frac{1}{k} |=|\sum_{k=n+1}^{n+p} \frac{1}{k}|= \\
> = \frac{1}{n+1}+\frac{1}{n+2}+\dots+\frac{1}{n+p}\geq p\cdot \frac{1}{n+p}
> \end{align}
> $$
> buď $n_{0}\in \mathbb{N}$ - lib: $\begin{align}n=n_{0}+1 \\  p=n_{0}+1\end{align}$ $(m=2n_{0}+p)$
> 
> $$
> \geq(n_{0}+1)\cdot \frac{1}{2n_{0}+2}=\frac{1}{2}=:\epsilon
> $$
