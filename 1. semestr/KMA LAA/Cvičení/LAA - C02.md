# Vydělte polynom (${}4x^5-11x^4+13x^{3}-26x^{2}+36x-22{}$) polynomem (${}x^{3}-x^{2}-5{}$) se zbytkem.
$$
\begin{align}
\frac{4x^5-11x^4+13x^{3}-26x^{2}+36x-22}{x^{3}-x^{2}-5}   &  &   4x^{2} \\
4x^5-11x^4+13x^{3}-26x^{2}+36x-22 - 4x^{2}(x^{3}-x^{2}-5)= \\
4x^5-11x^4+13x^{3}-26x^{2}+36x-22 - 4x^5+4x^4+20x^{2}= \\
-7x^4+13x^{3}-6x^{2}+36x-22 &  & -7x \\
-7x^4+13x^{3}-6x^{2}+36x-22 +7x(x^{3}-x^{2}-5)= \\
-7x^4+13x^{3}-6x^{2}+36x-22 +7x^4-7x^{3}-35x= \\
6x^{3}-6x+x-22 &  & 6 \\
6x^{3}-6x+x-22 - 6(x^{3}-x^{2}-5)= \\
6x^{3}-6x+x-22 -6x^{3}+6x^{2}+30= \\
x+8 \\
 &  & =4x^{2}-7x+6+\frac{x+8}{x^{3}-x^{2}-5}
\end{align}
$$
