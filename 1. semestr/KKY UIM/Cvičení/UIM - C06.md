> **Téma:** 
> **Koncepty:** [[Populační modely]]

- ${}r=2{}$
- ${}K=1000{}$


$$
\begin{align}
1=r\left( 1-\frac{x}{K} \right) \\
1=2\left( 1-\frac{x}{1000} \right) \\
1=2-\frac{x}{500} \\
\frac{x}{500}=1 \\
x^r_{1}:=x=500 =\boxed{K-\frac{K}{r}}\\
 \\
x^r_{2}=0
\end{align}
$$
